MODE TRANSITION

Two different meanings of “loss”

keep the metaphor honest
Predictive loss can jump when a long-stable source of input disappears while the internal transition model still expects it. Repeated mismatch can slowly reshape the dominant modes until ordinary continuation becomes predictable again.

Emotional loss is not reduced to an optimization scalar. The site only borrows the machine-learning word because the computational analogy is useful: the expected +1 continuation became badly wrong, then gradually less wrong.

A later cue can transiently reinstantiate part of the old mode without undoing the newer stable model. That is why temporal foveation can feel like another era becoming sharp while the current self remains present.
Working abstraction. R = ΔS − ΔŜ is the unexpected change. A decayed stamped memory M=(R,Z) remains mostly latent. Current context computes a match m=K(Ct,Z). A nonlinear knee yields a=σ(k(m−θ)). When a rises, the past state is partially reinstated while the present remains active: Oreflect=H(Snow,a·Spast). The whorl visual borrows one idea from the older ArtificialCortex work: structured coupling can make some states easier to revisit or pin. Here it is deliberately only a visualization/metaphor until an experiment earns more.
